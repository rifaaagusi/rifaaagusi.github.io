About me

Who I am

I'm a Visual Communication Designer based in Bekasi, Indonesia, working across graphic design, video editing, 2D animation, and motion graphics. I help brands and events communicate through strong visuals — from identity systems and print to social videos and logo animations.


I hold a Bachelor's degree in Visual Communication Design (GPA 3.52) from Universitas Indraprasta PGRI, and I'm highly proficient in English (TOEFL 617).
